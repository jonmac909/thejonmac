About

Jon Mac is a Canadian entrepreneur, business mentor, and online educator. As

the founder of Elevate Elite, Jon empowers aspiring creators and entrepreneurs to

build profitable online businesses through practical education and mentorship.

He’s passionate about helping others achieve financial freedom by teaching

proven systems for monetizing content and growing digital income streams.

Early life

Jon Mac was born and raised in Canada, growing up in a working-class family.

From an early age, Jon was driven by the desire to create financial freedom—not

just for himself, but for his family as well. He explored various side hustles,

teaching himself skills like marketing, web design, and eCommerce through trial

and error.

By his early twenties, Jon had already launched multiple successful online

businesses. He started with selling products online, tried affiliate marketing, and

eventually built his own eCommerce brands and software. Along the way, Jon

honed his expertise in paid advertising, content creation, and digital marketing.

Seeing the results of his work, he made the bold decision to pursue

entrepreneurship full-time, leaving behind the traditional career path to focus on

building his own companies.

Companies

Jon Mac is the founder of CommerceHQ, an eCommerce platform designed to simplify online store creation for entrepreneurs. He built the company from the ground up, scaling it into a multi-million dollar SaaS business by focusing on user-friendly technology and community-driven growth. After years of leading its success, Jon successfully exited CommerceHQ, using the experience and lessons learned to mentor others on building and scaling their own online businesses.

Elevate Elite

Building on his success with CommerceHQ, Jon Mac founded Elevate Elite, an

education and mentorship platform designed to help aspiring creators and

entrepreneurs. Elevate Elite teaches students how to start, grow, and scale

profitable online businesses by turning content views into real income through

ads, sponsorships, affiliate marketing, and digital products.
